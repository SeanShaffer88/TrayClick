TrayClick - portable build

Run the portable exe from anywhere; nothing is installed and no
admin rights are needed. Your settings live in
%APPDATA%\TrayClick\config.json and are shared with the installed
build, so you can switch between them freely.

To remove TrayClick, quit it from the tray menu and delete the exe
(delete the settings folder too if you want a clean slate). If you
turned on "Start with Windows" for the portable exe, turn it off
first (or toggle it once after moving the exe) so the sign-in entry
keeps pointing at the right place.

The build is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first
run: choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".
